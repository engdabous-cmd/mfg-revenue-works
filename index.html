01 / THE OPEN LOOP

What happens to quotes that never get a yes or no?

05 / KEEP THE INPUT SIMPLE

Commercial context.
Not technical files.

Customer aliases are welcome. A basic spreadsheet is enough to begin a discussion.

Download the blank intake template ↓

What helps

Quote ID, customer alias, quote date, last activity date, validity date, current status and a short non-sensitive note. Quoted value is optional. Mark unknowns explicitly.

What to leave out

No drawings, CAD, margins, passwords, bank details, customer contact lists or controlled technical data. Remove personal information and confidential part or program identifiers. Do not send export-controlled, regulated or otherwise restricted information.

How intake works

Start with an inquiry only. Do not attach real quote records to your first message. We agree the scope, permitted data, transfer method, processing tools, access and retention/deletion terms before you share an export. Confirm that you have permission to share it.

Data handling

This static website has no upload form, account system or analytics scripts. It does not receive or store your quote records. GitHub provides the hosting and may process ordinary connection information under its own policies. Email inquiries are handled through the email provider. No security certification or compliance guarantee is implied.

Automation may assist organization and drafting; recommendations receive human review. No confidential data is requested through this page. We will discuss any proposed processing tools before accepting your records.
